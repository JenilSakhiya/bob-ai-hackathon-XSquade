# Problem Statement

Modern enterprise Security Operations Centers (SOCs) face an overwhelming volume of disconnected security alerts every day. Analysts must manually triage hundreds of alerts across disparate consoles, leading to severe alert fatigue and long dwell times before actual threats are detected and contained.

CyberSentinel solves this by acting as an autonomous AI SOC assistant that ingests telemetry, correlates events into unified incidents, explains risk through transparent point scoring, formulates attack hypotheses, and recommends prioritized mitigation actions.
